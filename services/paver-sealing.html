Frequently Asked Paver Sealing Questions

How Long Does Paver Sealing Last?

It depends on sunlight, traffic, and the product used. In Palm Beach County, many homeowners plan for a maintenance coat in 18 to 36 months to keep protection and color at their best.

Will Sealing Make My Pavers Slippery?

Most breathable sealers are designed to maintain natural texture. For pool decks or smooth stone, we can add a fine traction additive to help improve grip.

Can You Remove Old Or Failing Sealer?

Often, yes. We can evaluate the surface and, when needed, use specialty removers and a careful cleaning plan to reduce whitening or uneven sheen before applying a new, compatible sealer.

What Is Paver Sealing?

Paver sealing is a protective finish applied to concrete, clay, or natural stone pavers after a thorough cleaning and re-sanding. The sealer helps lock out water, oil, and dirt, and it stabilizes joint sand to limit weed growth and ant intrusion. The right product also enhances color, reduces fading from the Florida sun, and makes routine cleaning easier. Think of it as a clear shield that preserves the beauty and life of your hardscapes.

Where Paver Sealing Works Best

  • Driveways and front walkways
  • Backyard patios and outdoor kitchens
  • Pool decks and screened lanais
  • Garden paths and sitting areas

Benefits Of Professional Paver Sealing

DIY sealing can seem simple, but results often vary. Our sealing and pressure washing pros use the right cleaners, rinsing methods, and sealers tailored to Florida conditions. Homeowners choose sealing for these clear wins:

  • Color revival that makes pavers look richer and more even
  • Protection from tire marks, leaf tannins, and irrigation stains
  • UV resistance to fight fading and chalking
  • Reduced weed growth and joint erosion with sand stabilization
  • Faster cleanups because spills and dirt sit on the surface
  • Longer paver life, which helps protect your investment

Results and re-coat timelines vary by surface type, exposure, and foot or vehicle traffic. We'll walk you through options and set clear expectations before we begin.

Our Proven Paver Sealing Process

Great sealing starts with spotless, dry pavers and joints filled to the proper depth. Here is how Canes Pressure Washing brings out the best in your hardscapes:

  1. Inspection and test area to match the right cleaner and sealer finish.
  2. Professional pressure washing using controlled pressure and surface cleaners to lift grime without etching.
  3. Joint sanding with quality polymeric or kiln-dried sand for stability and a finished look.
  4. Dry time monitoring to ensure ideal moisture levels before sealing.
  5. Application of premium breathable sealer in even coats for full coverage and a consistent sheen.

Most projects can be walked on within several hours. Vehicle traffic usually requires a longer cure. We will give you simple care tips so your surface stays beautiful.

Local Insight For Your Service Needs

Local sunny days, summer storms, and frequent irrigation can be tough on pavers. You might notice rust or hard-water spots near sprinklers, leaf stains under oaks, and mildew in shaded areas. In neighborhoods with pool decks and screen enclosures, moisture and sunscreen overspray can dull the surface. Our process targets these local troublemakers, then locks in a protective finish so your pavers stand up better to everyday wear and tear.

Local Tip: To reduce whitening or haze, choose a breathable sealer and let pavers dry fully after cleaning. If your pavers were recently installed, waiting a short period before sealing helps moisture escape and promotes a smoother, longer-lasting finish.

Finish Options That Fit Your Style

Every surface and taste is different. We offer finishes that keep a natural stone look or add a richer, enhanced tone. Not sure which you prefer? We can show examples and help you pick the look that fits your home's color scheme and landscaping.

Care And Maintenance After Sealing

Sealing does not end the need for care, but it makes maintenance easier. Use a gentle rinse as needed and spot clean spills early. Avoid harsh acids and wire brushes. If you pressure wash later, use low, controlled pressure to protect the finish and joint sand. Re-sealing schedules vary by sun exposure and traffic, but many homes plan a light refresh after a couple of years to keep protection at its best.

Perfect For Driveways, Patios, And Pool Decks

Whether you want a crisp front entry or a safer pool surround, sealing supports beauty and function. Sealed pavers resist hot-tire pickup and help reduce slipperiness when paired with the right product and surface texture.
