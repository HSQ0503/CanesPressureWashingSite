What A Complete Exterior Wash Includes

Your exterior needs the right mix of water pressure, detergents, and technique to clean without damage. Our team inspects every surface, chooses low or moderate pressure as needed, and uses targeted cleaners to lift organic growth, dirt, and rust stains. We rinse thoroughly so your property looks bright and welcoming when we finish.

  • Soft wash for siding, stucco, and painted surfaces to prevent etching
  • Concrete and driveway cleaning to cut through tire marks and oil spots
  • Paver cleaning with joint-friendly rinsing to remove sand, weeds, and algae
  • Roof wash using gentle methods for tile, shingle, and metal systems
  • Gutter brightening and rinse out when needed to clear light debris
  • Fence, deck, and screen enclosure washing for a uniform clean

We use eco-friendly detergents that are selected for the surface and rinsed thoroughly. This approach helps protect nearby landscaping while still delivering a deep clean.

Benefits You Can See Right Away

Clean surfaces do more than look good. They last longer because mold and grime trap moisture that can degrade finishes. A professional wash also boosts curb appeal for showings, HOA checks, and photo day. It is an affordable way to refresh high-traffic areas before you consider repainting or resurfacing.

If you want a single, dependable crew that treats your place with care, work with our local exterior cleaning team. We schedule efficiently, arrive prepared, and keep water management top of mind so your walkways and entries are ready to use soon after service. Trained technicians adjust pressure and flow for each surface to help prevent damage.

How Our Five-Step Cleaning Process Works

  1. Walkthrough: We note surface types, delicate areas, outlets, and drainage paths.
  2. Prep: We move light items, pre-wet plants, and protect sensitive fixtures.
  3. Apply: We apply targeted solutions to break down organic growth and grime.
  4. Rinse And Restore: We rinse with controlled pressure, section by section.
  5. Final Check: We spot-treat trouble areas and tidy up so everything is ready to enjoy.

Care tips we follow on every job include testing a small area first, keeping the wand moving to avoid stripes, and working top to bottom so dirt does not redeposit. After your wash, allow surfaces to dry fully before sealing, painting, or parking vehicles on cleaned concrete.

Local tip: Afternoon showers and sea breezes can redeposit salt and dust. Plan exterior cleaning earlier in the day when possible, and schedule maintenance washes before the summer rainy season for longer-lasting results.

Surfaces And Project Types We Handle

Every property has a mix of materials, each with different cleaning needs. Stucco and painted surfaces respond best to soft washing with a wide fan spray. Concrete handles a bit more pressure, but even then, we focus on flow and distance rather than force alone. Pavers benefit from a careful rinse that lifts grime without blasting out sand, and screen enclosures need low-pressure attention to avoid tearing mesh or bending frames.

We frequently clean pool decks, lanais, and patio furniture pads where sunscreen and foot traffic leave slick films. Driveways and curbs collect tire dust and oil that darken the surface, while gutters develop tiger stripes that make trim look older than it is. Roofs require a measured approach that respects underlayment and coatings. With each project, we match the method to the material so the finish looks bright and uniform.

Local Conditions We Clean Around

Coastal air carries salt that clings to glass, railings, and metal accents. Summer storms push leaf tannins and red clay across concrete, leaving brown or rusty discoloration. Irrigation overspray can create faint rust marks from well water, and shaded sides of homes often grow algae faster than sunny walls.

Barrel tile, coquina accents, and shell-based pavers are beautiful but porous, so they hold moisture and need gentle, thorough rinsing. Planning washes seasonally helps keep these surfaces from staining deeply, which can reduce how often you need heavier restoration work.

Questions Homeowners Ask Before Booking

How Often Should I Schedule Exterior Cleaning?

Most homes benefit from a wash every 6 to 12 months. Properties near trees, sprinklers, or busy roads may need more frequent touch-ups to control algae, dust, and water spots.

Will Pressure Washing Damage My Paint Or Stucco?

Not when the right method is used. We soft wash delicate finishes with low pressure and proper detergents, then rinse gently to protect texture and color.

Can You Remove Oil Stains And Rust From Concrete?

Yes, many stains lighten or lift with pretreatments and hot or cold rinsing. Some older or deeply set stains may not disappear completely, but they often improve significantly.

Do I Need To Be Home During The Service?

It helps for the walkthrough, but it is not required if we have access to outdoor faucets and the work areas. We will confirm details and send updates as needed.

What About Plants And Outdoor Furniture?

We pre-wet nearby plants, use targeted cleaners, and rinse thoroughly. Please move fragile items and cushions indoors before service so everything stays dry and tidy.
