What Is Roof Cleaning?

Roof cleaning is a careful wash designed to remove algae, moss, mildew, and dirt from shingles, tiles, and metal panels. In Palm Beach County's warm, humid climate, black streaks from algae can spread fast. High pressure is risky on roofs because it can loosen granules, lift edges, and force water where it should not go. That is why we rely on a low-pressure soft wash paired with the right cleaning solution. It breaks the bond between stains and the surface, then rinses clean with water.

Soft washing protects shingles and coatings while delivering a longer-lasting clean. You get a brighter roof and a lower chance of premature wear. Most homes can be cleaned the same day with minimal disruption.

Benefits Of Professional Roof Cleaning

A clean roof does more than look nice. It supports your home’s health and value. Here is what homeowners often notice after service:

  • Better curb appeal for listings, appraisals, and HOA compliance.
  • Longer roof life by clearing algae that can hold moisture against the surface.
  • Improved energy efficiency on light-colored tile or metal by restoring reflectivity.
  • Lower risk of leaks caused by trapped debris along hips, valleys, and flashing.
  • Less organic growth washing into gutters and downspouts.

Hiring a pro also means safer work. Roofs are steep and fragile in spots. Our team brings stable access, protective footwear, and controlled application tools to do the job right.

Our Safe Soft-Wash Process

Every roof is different. We tailor our approach to your material, age, and staining level. Here is how a typical visit goes:

  • Walkthrough and inspection to spot fragile areas, skylights, solar, or loose trim.
  • Set up safety gear and protect landscaping with pre-wetting and temporary covers as needed.
  • Apply a balanced cleaning solution at low pressure to break down algae and dirt.
  • Allow proper dwell time so stains release without scrubbing.
  • Gentle rinse to carry away residue and reveal an even, bright finish.
  • Final check and clean-up of walkways, plants, and work areas.

Most standard roofs take two to four hours, depending on size, pitch, accessibility, and the level of buildup. Heavier growth or delicate tile may require a little more time for a complete, even result.

Pro tip for local homeowners: after spring pollen and summer storms, algae can return quickly on shaded sides facing heavy oak canopies. Scheduling a soft wash right before the wettest months helps keep stains from taking hold and can reduce total maintenance over the year. Timing and cost vary by roof size, material, and seasonal demand.

What To Expect And How To Prepare

We make getting pressure washing excellence simpler than ever. A little prep goes a long way to a smooth service day:

  • Close all windows and doors; check screens are seated.
  • Move vehicles away from eaves and garage doors.
  • Cover or move delicate patio items and outdoor cushions.
  • Keep pets indoors; let us know if gates are locked.
  • Note any irrigation timers so we can avoid overlap during the visit.

On the day of service, you do not need to climb or assist on the roof. We will handle the setup, wash, and full cleanup. The weather can shift in Palm Beach County, so if a storm rolls in, we may pause and resume once it passes to maintain quality and even drying. If wind or lightning makes work unsafe, we will reschedule promptly at a time that suits you.

How Often Should You Clean Your Roof?

Most homes benefit from roof cleaning every one to three years. The exact schedule depends on shade, tree cover, roof material, and how quickly algae returns. Lighter maintenance at the first sign of streaks makes future cleanings faster and helps you stay ahead of HOA reminders. If you are unsure, we are happy to take a look and give you a straightforward recommendation based on your roof's condition.

Frequently Asked Roof Cleaning Questions

Is Pressure Washing Safe For My Roof?

High-pressure washing is not recommended on shingles or most tiles because it can strip granules, open small gaps, and shorten roof life. Our soft-wash method uses low pressure and a targeted solution to loosen stains, then a gentle rinse to carry them away. It delivers a deep clean without the risks of blasting water into seams.

Will The Cleaning Harm My Plants Or Outdoor Spaces?

We take plant care seriously. Before we start, we pre-wet the surrounding beds and grass. During the wash, we use controlled application to limit drift and avoid runoff pooling. Afterward, we rinse the area and check for any residue. These steps help protect your landscaping while still giving the roof the attention it needs.

How Long Does It Take, And What Does It Cost?

Most projects finish the same day, often within two to four hours. Timing and pricing vary by roof size, pitch, access, material, and the level of staining.
