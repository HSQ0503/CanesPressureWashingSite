What Is House Washing?

House washing is the careful cleaning of your home's exterior using a low-pressure application of biodegradable detergents followed by a controlled rinse. Unlike high-pressure blasting, soft washing treats the root of the problem by loosening organic growth so it rinses away safely. This method protects painted surfaces, stucco, vinyl, Hardie board, brick, trim, and sealants while restoring curb appeal.

Our trained technicians adjust solutions and dwell time based on your home's materials and the level of buildup. The result is a cleaner exterior that stays bright longer and resists the quick return of algae and mildew common in Central Florida's warm, humid climate.

Our Safe Soft Wash Process

Every home is different. Here's how our team delivers consistent, safe results:

  • Inspection: We walk the property, note oxidation, fragile trim, and areas of heavy growth.
  • Protection: We pre-wet plants, cover sensitive fixtures, and move outdoor items as needed.
  • Application: We apply a customized detergent blend at low pressure to break down grime.
  • Dwell: Solutions work for several minutes to lift contaminants without scrubbing.
  • Gentle Rinse: We rinse with controlled pressure for an even, spot-free finish.
  • Detailing: We touch up window frames, doorways, and high-touch areas before a final check.
Local tip: Irrigation overspray can leave mineral or rust stains on siding. Rinse exterior walls after lawn watering when possible and schedule house washing just before peak pollen in spring for the longest-lasting results.

Soft Wash Vs. High-Pressure Cleaning

High pressure has its place on hard surfaces like concrete, but it can scar siding, drive water behind trim, and remove paint. Soft washing uses low pressure with targeted detergents to clean effectively without unnecessary force. It's the preferred method for siding, soffits, fascia, and painted surfaces because it protects the finish while removing stains at the source.

How Often Should You Schedule House Washing?

Most local homeowners find the best rhythm is every 6 to 12 months. Shady lots, homes near water, and properties with heavy tree cover may need more frequent cleanings. If you are preparing to paint, list your home, or host an event, a single visit a few days in advance can make a big difference.

Care For Common Siding Types

Stucco

Stucco benefits from soft washing because it lifts algae from textured surfaces without scarring. We adjust detergents to reduce shadowing and streaks.

Vinyl And Painted Wood

We use low-pressure rinsing to avoid forcing water behind panels or under trim. Detergents break down grime so the rinse can be gentle.

Brick And Hardie Board

These surfaces clean up well with a tailored mix and careful rinsing to protect mortar lines and fiber cement coatings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Soft Washing Harm My Plants Or Lawn?

We protect landscaping by pre-wetting vegetation, controlling runoff, and rinsing thoroughly after treatment. Our detergents are applied at safe, job-specific dilutions and followed by a full rinse to leave your landscape in great shape.

How Long Do Results Last?

With our climate, most homes stay bright for 6 to 12 months. Shaded sides may show growth sooner. Our process slows the return of algae by treating it at the source and rinsing evenly for a clean, uniform finish.

Do I Need To Be Home For The Appointment?

No. If we have exterior access and your windows and gates are secured, we can complete the job and send photos upon request. We will confirm details ahead of time and share any notes after the service.
