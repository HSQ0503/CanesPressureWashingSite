What Is Commercial Pressure Washing?

Maintaining a pristine exterior is vital for any business, as your property’s appearance is the first thing clients and tenants notice. Our commercial pressure washing service utilizes an expert-grade combination of calibrated water pressure, high-heat systems, and industrial-strength detergents to tackle the most stubborn contaminants. We specialize in the complete removal of embedded dirt, slick algae, and unsightly mildew, as well as difficult-to-treat stains like chewing gum, automotive grease, and irrigation rust. By deep-cleaning your hard surfaces, we don’t just improve aesthetics; we also mitigate slip-and-fall hazards, creating a safer environment for everyone.

Recognizing that every building material requires a unique approach, we provide specialized care for more sensitive exteriors. For delicate surfaces such as stucco, EIFS (synthetic stucco), and painted metal, we employ a professional "soft washing" technique. This method replaces high-velocity force with high-volume, low-pressure water streams and targeted, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that neutralize organic growth at the root. This ensures a thorough, long-lasting deep clean that protects your property's finishes and structural integrity without the risk of etching or water intrusion. Whether you manage a retail storefront or a large industrial complex, our tailored methods ensure your investment remains protected and professional.

Our Step-By-Step Cleaning Process

  1. Site Walkthrough: We evaluate surfaces, stains, drainage, and sensitive areas like landscaping and electrical.
  2. Pre-Treatment: We apply targeted cleaners for algae, gum, grease, or rust to break down buildup.
  3. Wash Method: We select hot water pressure washing or soft washing based on the material.
  4. Detail Work: We edge clean curbs, corners, and baseboards where grime collects.
  5. Rinse and Neutralize: We rinse thoroughly and balance surfaces as needed.
  6. Final Check: We do a walkthrough with you to confirm results.

Frequently Asked Commercial Pressure Washing Questions

How Often Should A Business Schedule Commercial Pressure Washing?

Most storefronts benefit from quarterly sidewalk cleaning and a building wash once or twice per year. High-traffic locations like restaurants and grocery stores may need monthly touch-ups on entry pads. The right schedule varies by material, shade, and foot traffic.

Can You Clean While We Are Open?

Yes, in many cases. We can cone off small areas and work in sections. For busier sites, we recommend early morning or evening service to keep customers flowing. Let us know your operating hours, and we will plan accordingly.

Will Pressure Washing Damage Stucco Or Paint?

Not when it is done correctly. We use soft washing for stucco and painted surfaces. This method relies on low pressure and the right cleaning solution to lift stains without stripping finishes.
