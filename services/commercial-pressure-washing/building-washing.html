What Professional Building Washing Includes

Building washing is a complete exterior cleaning for your home or low-rise property. It targets mildew, algae, pollen, spider webs, and grime on surfaces like stucco, painted siding, vinyl, brick, stone, fascia, and soffits. As a pressure washing provider, we adjust pressure to match each surface and rely on a gentle soft wash for delicate areas. That means a cleaner finish and longer-lasting results.

  • Soft wash for painted, vinyl, and stucco surfaces
  • Balanced cleaners that break down organic growth
  • Rinses that protect plants, windows, and fixtures
  • Spot attention for high-traffic corners and overhangs

Benefits Of Building Washing For Local Homes

Regular washing does more than refresh your paint. It helps protect your investment and keep your family comfortable.

  • Boost curb appeal before photos, parties, or sale
  • Reduce algae and mildew that can stain or smell
  • Extend the life of paint, stucco, and caulking
  • Clear pollen and dust that cling to shaded walls
  • Remove cobwebs and wasp marks around entries

Humidity in Florida fuels fast algae growth, especially on the shaded sides of homes in neighborhoods in the area. A scheduled wash keeps walls bright and prevents deep staining that is harder to remove later.

Frequently Asked Building Washing Questions

How Often Should I Schedule Building Washing?

Most homes do well with an annual wash. If your home is shaded by large oaks or faces a lake breeze, algae may return faster on one side. In those cases, plan a quick mid-year touch-up for that wall.

Is Soft Washing Safe For My Paint And Plants?

Yes. We pre-rinse landscaping, use balanced cleaners, and rinse thoroughly. Soft washing relies on dwell time rather than force, which protects paint, stucco, and caulking. Your greenery gets a final freshwater rinse before we leave.

How Long Does A Typical Building Wash Take?

Time varies by size and materials. A single-story home is often completed within a few hours, while larger properties may take longer. We provide a clear window when you book so you can plan your day.
