What Is Professional Driveway Cleaning?

Professional driveway cleaning uses the right pressure, flow, and detergents to remove grime without damaging your surface. We combine a surface cleaner for even results with targeted pretreatments to break down oil, leaf tannins, rust, and algae. The outcome is a brighter, safer, and easier-to-maintain driveway.

How Our Driveway Cleaning Process Works

We follow a clear, careful process designed for Florida driveways and our local climate.

  • Assessment: We inspect concrete or pavers, note stains, and check drainage and landscaping.
  • Pretreat: Targeted solutions loosen oil, algae, rust, and leaf stains while protecting nearby plants.
  • Deep Clean: A professional surface cleaner delivers even, streak-free results across the entire slab.
  • Detail Work: We rinse edges, garage thresholds, joints, and hard-to-reach corners.
  • Post-Treatment: A light application discourages fast algae regrowth in our warm, humid weather.
  • Optional Add-Ons: Paver sand refresh and sealing recommendations if requested. Pricing varies by size and condition.

Frequently Asked Driveway Cleaning Questions

How Often Should I Clean My Driveway?

Most homes do well with a professional cleaning once a year. Shaded or heavily irrigated areas may benefit from a quick refresh every 6 to 9 months. We can set a reminder schedule that fits your property.

Will Pressure Washing Damage My Concrete Or Pavers?

Used correctly, pressure washing is safe. We balance pressure and water flow, use surface cleaners for uniform coverage, and choose detergents that do the heavy lifting so we do not rely on excessive force. For pavers, we protect joint sand and can discuss light resanding if needed.

Can You Remove Oil And Rust Stains Completely?

Many stains lift significantly, and some disappear. Results depend on how long the stain has set and the material type. We pretreat and, when helpful, return for a second pass. We will set clear expectations during the on-site assessment.
