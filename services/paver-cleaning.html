What Is Professional Paver Cleaning?

Paver cleaning is a careful, step-by-step service that lifts embedded dirt, mold, and stains from concrete, brick, and natural stone pavers. Unlike DIY methods that can leave streaks or etch the surface, we combine specialized detergents with controlled pressure and a surface cleaner to wash evenly. The goal is simple: restore color, protect the surface, and get your space ready for everyday living or optional sealing.

Our Paver Cleaning Process

Every property is different, so we start with a short walk-through and set clear expectations. Here is how our typical service works:

  1. Inspection and Protection: We inspect the paver type, stains, and joint condition, then protect nearby landscaping and fixtures.
  2. Targeted Pretreatments: We apply paver-safe detergents to lift algae, tire marks, and organic stains like leaf tannins.
  3. Even Surface Cleaning: A professional surface cleaner delivers consistent results across driveways, patios, and pool decks without striping.
  4. Detail Rinsing: We gently rinse edges, borders, and tight spots for a uniform finish.
  5. Joint TLC: As needed, we brush debris from joints and discuss re-sanding options to stabilize pavers before an optional seal.
Local tip: Florida humidity feeds algae fast. Booking paver cleaning in early spring or right after the rainy season can keep surfaces brighter and less slippery when outdoor time ramps up.

Frequently Asked Paver Cleaning Questions

How Often Should I Schedule Paver Cleaning?

Most homes benefit from professional cleaning every 12 to 18 months. High-shade areas or pool decks that stay damp may need service a bit sooner. We can recommend a plan after seeing your space.

Will Pressure Washing Damage My Pavers?

Used correctly, pressure washing is safe for pavers. We rely on detergent dwell time and a surface cleaner for even results, then finish with a gentle rinse. Avoiding high-pressure wands on joints prevents unnecessary sand loss.

Do You Re-Sand Joints?

Yes, if the joints need it. After cleaning and drying, we can re-sand and discuss optional sealing. Final recommendations vary by paver type, exposure, and how you use the area.
