Frequently Asked Window Cleaning Questions

How Often Should I Schedule Window Cleaning?

Most homes in Palm Beach County do well with cleaning every 4 to 6 months. If sprinklers hit the glass or trees shed heavy pollen, consider a quarterly plan to keep buildup away.

Will Cleaning Scratch My Glass Or Damage The Frames?

No. We avoid abrasive pads, use soft brushes, and rinse with purified water. Frames and seals are treated gently, and we protect surrounding surfaces as we work.

Do I Need To Be Home During The Service?

Someone should be present for the interior work and the final walkthrough. For exterior-only cleanings, we can proceed with your permission and text updates as needed.

What Professional Window Cleaning Includes

Professional window cleaning is more than a quick wipe with store-bought cleaners. We remove grime, dust, and hard-water buildup from your glass and detail the frames, tracks, and screens so daylight pours in clean and bright. As a local pressure washing and exterior cleaning team, we know which tools are safe for Florida homes and how to care for different window types without risk.

  • Interior and exterior glass cleaned for a clear, even shine
  • Soft-brush agitation to lift dirt from frames and sills
  • Track detailing to remove grit, cobwebs, and debris
  • Screen rinse and light scrub to restore airflow and clarity
  • Spot treatment for sprinkler stains and stubborn marks

Clean windows do more than look nice. They brighten rooms, improve curb appeal, and help protect your investment. Homeowners see real differences after a professional cleaning.

  • Longer glass life by removing grit and minerals that can etch
  • Safer results by avoiding ladders and harsh chemicals
  • A healthier indoor environment by clearing dust and pollen around frames

Your windows will shine without streaks or residue. You get a tidy, respectful crew and results you can see the moment you step inside.

How Our Window Cleaning Process Works

1) Quick, Courteous Scheduling

Call 561-537-5674 and tell us what you need. We'll provide a fast, no-pressure estimate that varies by window count, access, and buildup.

2) Careful Prep And Protection

We place drop cloths where needed, move small items with care, and wear shoe covers indoors. Plants and landscaping near windows are protected before we start.

3) Safe Cleaning, Inside And Out

We use gentle scrubbers and purified water for a spot-free rinse on exterior glass. Indoors, we work by hand for a flawless finish. We never use high pressure on glass.

4) Detailing And Final Walkthrough

Tracks and sills are wiped, screens are rinsed, and edges are checked in different light so you get a consistent, streak-free result. Before we leave, we walk the property with you to confirm everything looks great.

Local Tip: Florida sprinkler lines can leave mineral spots on lower windows. Angle sprinkler heads away from glass and schedule cleaning before peak summer heat to reduce new spotting and streaking.

Common Issues We Solve

Hard-Water And Sprinkler Spots

Mineral rings and cloudy patches build up where sprinklers hit the glass. We treat these areas carefully and recommend simple maintenance steps to slow new buildup.

Pollen, Dust, And Storm Residue

Seasonal pollen and afternoon storms can leave a film that a paper towel can't fix. Our purified-water rinse and soft scrub remove residue without streaks.

Screen And Track Grime

Tracks collect sand and grit, and screens trap dust. We clean both so the windows glide properly, and the air flows clean.
