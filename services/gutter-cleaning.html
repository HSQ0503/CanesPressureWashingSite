What Professional Gutter Cleaning Includes

Gutters catch rain from your roof and send it away from your home. When leaves, needles, and grit build up, water overflows and can stain siding, rot wood, or find its way inside. Our team removes all debris by hand where needed, checks downspouts, flushes the system, and makes sure water flows the right way. As a pressure washing provider, we also use gentle rinsing methods on exterior surfaces near your gutters so the area looks clean when we are done.

Every visit focuses on safety, cleanliness, and a tidy job site. We protect landscaping, cover sensitive areas when needed, and bag debris for proper disposal. If we see small issues like a loose hanger or a crooked elbow, we will point them out and let you know your options.

Why Gutter Cleaning Matters

Florida weather can shift quickly. Afternoon showers are common, and heavy downpours often occur during hurricane season. Homes shaded by oaks and other trees collect leaves and twigs that settle into gutters. Pollen and roof granules can also form a dense layer that slows water. When the system cannot keep up, water spills over the sides, pools near your slab, and can lead to staining, soft soil, and foundation stress. Inside the gutters, standing water can attract mosquitoes and accelerate rust or seam wear.

Routine cleaning helps your gutters work the way they should on old and new homes alike. If you have a screened enclosure, a metal roof, or multiple rooflines, a planned cleaning schedule keeps things simple before the next big storm rolls through.

Our Safe, Step-By-Step Process

1) Inspection And Setup

We start with a quick walkaround to note roof pitch, gutter material, and downspout locations. Ladders are placed on stable ground and tied off where needed. We protect plants and set out debris bags.

2) Debris Removal

We remove leaves, sticks, and roof grit by hand or with specialized tools. Corners and behind hangers receive extra attention because these are common clog points.

3) Downspout Check And Flush

Downspouts are the heart of the system. We check for blockages at elbows and ground-level bends, then flush the full run to confirm flow. If an underground drain is present, we test entry flow and clear minor clogs at the connection.

4) Rinse And Light Wash

As a pressure washing company, we use a controlled, low-pressure rinse around the gutter exterior to remove residue and streaks without damage. Your gutters are cleaned safely without damage to shingles, seams, or landscaping.

5) Final Review

We confirm pitch, check for drips at joints, and ensure downspouts discharge away from the foundation. The work area is left neat and your pathway is clear.

Local Tip: After major storms, check your downspout outlets for mulch and palm fronds that can block flow. If water pools near the splash block, call 561-537-5674 for a quick checkup before the next rain.

How Often Should You Clean Gutters?

Most homes in our area do well with service twice a year, usually in late spring and again in fall. Homes under heavy tree cover or near construction may need more frequent visits. The right schedule varies by roof size, pitch, and the number of trees around your lot. We can recommend a plan after a short visit, so you only pay for what you need.

Frequently Asked Gutter Cleaning Questions

How Much Does Gutter Cleaning Cost?

Pricing depends on home size, stories, roof pitch, gutter length, and debris level. Corner-heavy layouts and tall rooflines take more time. After a quick look, we provide a clear estimate so you know the exact cost before we begin.

Do You Clean The Outside Of The Gutters Too?

Yes. During your service, we perform a careful rinse and light wash on the exterior faces to reduce streaks and buildup. If you want a deeper exterior brightening, we can add a targeted wash to remove stubborn tiger stripes where possible.

Can You Service Homes With Gutter Guards?

We work with many types of screens and covers. Some systems require panel removal to access the troughs. We will explain the process and handle reassembly so the guard system keeps doing its job.
