What Patio Cleaning Includes

Patio cleaning is a targeted exterior wash designed to lift organic growth, dirt, and stains from outdoor living spaces. For concrete and pavers, our pressure washing uses controlled PSI and a surface cleaner for even results. For delicate materials like travertine, tile, or painted concrete, we use a soft wash approach that relies on low pressure and carefully balanced detergents. The goal is a refreshed patio that looks great, feels safe to walk on, and is ready for family time or guests.

Common surfaces we clean around include:

  • Concrete patios and walkways with leaf and rust stains
  • Brick paver patios with sanded joints and polymeric sand
  • Screened lanais and pool decks with algae buildup
  • Travertine and tile areas that require a gentler wash

Benefits Of Professional Patio Cleaning

A pro clean does more than brighten the space. It helps prevent premature wear and keeps everyone safer.

Our methods are designed for Florida sun, frequent showers, and sprinkler overspray. We focus on steady, even cleaning so you do not see those swirl marks that happen with rushed, high-pressure passes.

How Our Patio Cleaning Process Works

We keep the process simple and respectful of your property and schedule.

  1. Assessment: We identify your surface type, any existing sealer, and note stains like rust, tannins, or irrigation marks.
  2. Protection: Furniture and plants are moved or covered as needed. We secure doors and outlets and review water access.
  3. Pretreat: We apply a surface-safe detergent to loosen grime, algae, and mildew so we can use the lowest effective pressure.
  4. Even Cleaning: A professional surface cleaner delivers consistent results without streaks. Edges are detailed with a wand.
  5. Rinse and Post-Treat: A final rinse clears residue. Where needed, we apply a light post-treatment to slow regrowth.
  6. Walkthrough: We review the results with you and share care tips for longer-lasting cleanliness.
Local tip: During our rainy season, growth can return fast on shaded patios. Scheduling cleanings just after heavy pollen drops or peak summer showers helps reduce repeat buildup and can lower overall maintenance costs.

Local Know-How For Patios

Florida patios face a few predictable challenges. Warm, humid air fuels algae and mildew, especially in north-facing and shaded areas. Oak and citrus trees drop tannin-rich leaves that can stain porous surfaces. Sprinkler overspray can cause orange rust marks. The wrong pressure or detergent can set stains deeper or strip joint sand between pavers.

Here is how we address local issues:

  • Organic Growth: Balanced pretreatments break down algae so we can clean with lower pressure.
  • Tannin and Leaf Stains: Targeted spot treatments reduce discoloration without whitening the surrounding area.
  • Rust Stains: Specialty removers are used carefully to avoid etching or color changes.
  • Joint Sand Care: We avoid blasting sand from paver joints and explain what to expect if joints are already low or loose.

Every patio is different. We give you clear expectations before we start and practical aftercare tips to help the clean last longer. To talk through your specific surface, reach out to Canes Pressure Washing at 561-537-5674.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Often Should I Schedule Patio Cleaning In Florida?

Most local area homeowners schedule patio cleaning once or twice a year. Shady, screen-enclosed, or heavily used areas may benefit from a light maintenance clean every 4 to 6 months. Timing can vary by surface, tree coverage, and sprinkler exposure. 

Is Pressure Washing Safe For Pavers And Travertine?

Yes, when done correctly. We set pressure based on the material and use a surface cleaner for even passes. For travertine and sealed pavers, we often choose a soft wash with low pressure and detergent. The goal is to clean the surface without dislodging joint sand, lifting sealer, or causing etching.

How Long Does A Typical Patio Cleaning Take, And Do I Need To Prep?

Smaller patios can take an hour or less. Larger pool decks and lanais may take several hours. Please remove small items and clear the area as best you can. We can help move light furniture if needed and will protect nearby plants and fixtures during the service.
