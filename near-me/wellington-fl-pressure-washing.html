Wellington Pressure Washing: Common Issues We Solve

Wellington’s warm, coastal climate and frequent afternoon showers create ideal conditions for mold, mildew, and algae. Sprinklers pulling from wells can leave rust and mineral stains on walls and sidewalks, while shaded entries and screen enclosures often grow slick organic films. After windy days, dust from nearby equestrian routes can settle on patios and driveways, dulling hardscapes across Wellington neighborhoods.

  • Black algae streaks on barrel tile and shingle roofs
  • Green mildew on stucco, Hardie siding, and soffits
  • Rust and irrigation stains on walls, pavers, and curbs
  • Slick pool cages and screen enclosures from organic buildup
  • Oil spots and tire marks on driveways and HOA sidewalks

Local tip: Late spring through fall brings regular rain that fuels algae growth. Booking exterior cleaning ahead of the wettest months helps surfaces stay brighter longer and reduces slip hazards on walkways.

Wellington Roof Cleaning You Can Trust

Tile and shingle roofs in Wellington often show black streaks caused by algae that thrive in heat and humidity. Our roof cleaning approach uses a controlled, low-pressure application to remove organic growth without forcing water under tiles or damaging shingles. The result is a brighter roof, better curb appeal, and a surface that stays cleaner for longer between maintenance visits.

Services Offered In Wellington, FL

Pressure washing

Our pressure washing tackles driveways, pavers, pool decks, fences, and more with the right combination of pressure and detergents. We adjust methods for each surface, helping Wellington homeowners and property managers safely and efficiently cut through years of grime.

Roof cleaning

Roof cleaning removes algae, mold, and lichen from tile and shingle roofs common across Wellington communities. We favor a soft-wash technique to protect delicate materials while restoring color and improving the look of your entire property.

House washing

House washing is a gentler exterior clean designed for painted stucco, Hardie board, and vinyl. Our detergents lift organic growth and staining so your home looks fresh without the risk of high-pressure damage to siding, seals, or trim.

Commercial pressure washing
