Our Exterior Cleaning Process

We keep things simple and thorough, so you know what to expect from start to finish.

  1. Inspect: We walk the property, note stains and delicate areas, and confirm water access.
  2. Protect: We pre-wet plants, cover sensitive fixtures, and move light items out of spray paths.
  3. Treat: We apply the right detergent, dwell briefly, and agitate only where needed.
  4. Rinse and Review: We rinse in sections, spot-treat remaining marks, and do a final walkthrough with you.

The result is a fresher home exterior, brighter concrete, and a roof that looks well cared for. If something needs touch-up, we handle it before we leave. We also offer commercial pressure washing!
