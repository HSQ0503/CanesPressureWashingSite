Soft Washing Vs. High Pressure

Not every surface needs high pressure. In fact, many do better with a soft wash that uses low pressure and smart detergents. This is the best method for roofs, painted siding, and delicate trim. High pressure belongs on concrete and some hardscapes. We choose the right process based on your home's materials so you get a deep clean without damage. It is the smart way to care for Windermere homes that face sun, rain, and lake humidity.
