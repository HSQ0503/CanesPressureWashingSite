Common Exterior Problems In Palm Beach Gardens, FL

Our coastal environment creates unique cleaning challenges:

  • Algae and mildew thrive in humidity and shaded spots under palms and live oaks, streaking siding, soffits, and screen enclosures.
  • Salt spray and coastal breezes can leave film on windows, railings, and commercial facades, dulling finishes over time.
  • Black roof streaks from algae can reduce curb appeal and make tile or asphalt shingles run hotter in the sun.
  • Irrigation overspray may leave rust or mineral marks on curbs, pavers, and garage doors.
  • Paver efflorescence and pool deck grime build quickly with afternoon storms and frequent outdoor living.

Local Tip: The rainy season typically brings afternoon downpours and higher humidity. Scheduling roof cleaning and house washing before summer helps prevent rapid regrowth and keeps gutters flowing when storms roll in.
