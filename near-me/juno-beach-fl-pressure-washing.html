Common Exterior Issues In Juno Beach

Homes near the shore face more than just sand tracked onto the driveway. Salt crystals carried by ocean breezes cling to glass, railings, and exterior paint. They trap moisture and speed up corrosion, especially on metal fixtures and patio furniture. Add in tropical sun and summer downpours, and you’ll see rapid algae growth on north-facing walls, shaded sidewalks, and screened lanais. Tile and shingle roofs often develop dark streaks caused by algae, while sprinklers using well water can leave orange rust marks on curbs and garage doors. These are everyday challenges for property owners in Juno Beach, and we tailor our process to address each one.

Because Juno Beach properties face constant humidity and salt, a planned schedule keeps surfaces looking their best.
