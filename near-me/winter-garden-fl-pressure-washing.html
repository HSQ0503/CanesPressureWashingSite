Frequently Asked Questions About Pressure Washing In Winter Garden

How often should I schedule exterior cleaning?

In our humid climate, many homes benefit from a light wash every 12 to 18 months. Shaded areas, north-facing walls, and pool decks may need attention sooner.

Will pressure washing damage my paint or plants?

We use soft washing for painted and delicate surfaces and rinse plants before, during, and after treatment. The goal is a safer clean with no peeling or burning.

What about pavers with sand between the joints?

We lower the pressure, use surface cleaners, and avoid direct wand blasting that can dislodge sand. If needed, we can discuss a sand refresh after cleaning.

Can you remove orange sprinkler stains and oil spots?

Many rust and oil stains respond to specialty pretreatments. Results vary by age and severity, but pre-soaking and dwell time help lift more discoloration.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. As long as we have access to water and the exterior areas, we can proceed and share updates. We confirm details with you before starting.
