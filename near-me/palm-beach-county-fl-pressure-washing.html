Common Exterior Issues In Palm Beach County

Warm, humid air encourages mildew on stucco, screen enclosures, and pool decks. Black streaks on shingle and tile roofs are often algae that thrive after summer showers. Along A1A and the Intracoastal Waterway, salt spray can leave a chalky film on windows, railings, and Hardie boards. Irrigation overspray leaves rust marks on walls and driveways, and tropical plants shed tannins that stain concrete and pavers after heavy rain. Our approach targets each of these issues with the right mix of water pressure, surfactants, and dwell time so the surface looks clean and stays that way longer.

Local tip: Book exterior cleaning before hurricane season peaks. Clearing gutters, rinsing screens, and scheduling roof cleaning help water drain properly during late‑summer downpours and reduce debris staining afterward.

Services We Offer In Palm Beach County

Pressure Washing

Pressure washing removes grime from driveways, sidewalks, patios, and pool decks without leaving streaks. We adjust PSI for each surface, then rinse with even passes so pavers, concrete, and travertine look bright again. This is ideal before sealing pavers or listing a home, especially in high‑traffic areas like courtyards and front entries.

Roof Cleaning

Roof cleaning is performed with a safe soft‑wash process that treats algae on asphalt shingles, clay barrel tile, and concrete tile. We use low pressure and a controlled detergent blend to lift stains without lifting granules or flooding attics. The result is a brighter roof that enhances curb appeal and helps reflect heat more effectively.

House Washing

House washing restores stucco, painted wood, and fiber cement siding. We rinse from the soffits down, protect outlets and cameras, and use a gentle solution to break up mildew near shaded sides of the home. Window frames and sills are detailed to minimize water spots once everything dries.

Safe Soft Wash Methods For Delicate Surfaces

Not every surface can handle high pressure. We soft wash roofs, painted trim, screen cages, and coquina stone to prevent etching and water intrusion. Our team blocks downspouts, diverts runoff from planters, and applies post‑rinses on sensitive landscaping. This careful process is ideal for Palm Beach County homes with lush tropical beds, courtyard fountains, and outdoor kitchens where overspray must be controlled.

Palm Beach County Roof Cleaning: What To Expect

Before roof cleaning, we inspect tiles for cracks and note fragile ridge caps. We keep technicians off delicate areas whenever possible by working from ladders and harness points. After applying the soft‑wash solution, stains fade over the next 24 to 48 hours. We finish with a clean perimeter rinse so fascia, gutters, and walkways look neat. Many clients pair this with house washing or driveway pressure washing for a complete refresh in a single visit.

Palm Beach County Pressure Washing: When To Schedule

Most homes benefit from pressure washing every 6 to 12 months. Coastal properties often require more frequent rinsing due to salt spray and windblown sand. Consider booking just before major holidays, real estate photos, or HOA inspections. If you run a storefront, aim for monthly or quarterly service so entrances, curbs, and dumpster pads never fall behind.
