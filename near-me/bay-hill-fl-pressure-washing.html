Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule pressure washing in Bay Hill?

Most homes benefit from a house wash every 12 months. Roof cleaning is often required every 2-3 years, though shaded homes may need attention sooner.

Is soft washing safe for my plants and paint?

Yes. We pre-wet vegetation, use controlled mixes, and rinse thoroughly. Soft washing protects paint, stucco, and roof shingles while removing stains.

Can you remove rust or orange sprinkler stains?

In many cases, yes. We have specialty treatments for rust and well-water mineral stains on concrete and walls. Severity and surface type guide the approach.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. As long as we have exterior access and water is on, we can complete most work and send updates.
