Our Services

How Can We Help You?

Roof Cleaning

Your roof is your home’s first line of defense, but it is also a magnet for unsightly black streaks and damaging organic growth. We utilize a specialized soft-wash technique that kills algae, lichen, and moss at the source without the risk of high-pressure damage to your shingles. This process not only restores the original color of your roof but also prevents premature aging and costly structural repairs.

By removing heat-absorbing dark stains, we can even help improve your home's energy efficiency during those hot Florida summers. Our professional roof cleaning ensures your rooftop stays cleaner for longer while preserving its full lifespan.

Learn More About Roof Cleaning

House Washing

Standard pressure washing can be too aggressive for siding, often leading to water intrusion or surface damage. We employ a low-pressure soft-wash method combined with eco-friendly cleaning agents to gently lift away dirt, cobwebs, and environmental pollutants. This comprehensive approach sanitizes your home’s exterior, effectively neutralizing mold and mildew spores that thrive in our humid climate.

Whether your home is finished in vinyl, brick, or stucco, our process restores its vibrant curb appeal safely and effectively. You can trust our team to treat your residence with the care it deserves, leaving behind a sparkling, refreshed finish.

Learn More About House Washing

Paver Sealing

Stone and brick pavers are a significant investment that requires specialized care to maintain their structural integrity and beauty. Our professional sealing process locks out moisture and prevents the growth of weeds and moss in the joints between stones. By applying high-quality sealants, we enhance the natural colors of your pavers while providing a protective barrier against UV rays and oil spills.

This treatment also stabilizes the joint sand, preventing erosion and shifting over time. The result is a durable, "just-installed" look that simplifies your long-term maintenance routine.

Patio Cleaning

Your outdoor living space should be a pristine sanctuary for relaxation and entertainment, not a collection point for grime and mildew. Our deep-cleaning process removes stubborn stains from concrete, stone, or tile surfaces, making your patio look brand new again. We pay close attention to high-traffic areas and corners where debris tends to accumulate, ensuring a uniform and thorough clean.

By eliminating slippery organic buildup, we also make your outdoor areas safer for family and guests. Let us transform your weathered patio into the perfect backdrop for your next weekend gathering.

Window Cleaning

Crystal-clear windows do more than improve your view; they allow natural light to brighten your entire interior. We go beyond a simple rinse, using professional-grade tools and purified water to eliminate streaks, smudges, and hard water deposits. Our team meticulously cleans frames, sills, and tracks to ensure that every part of your window system is functioning and looking its best.

This attention to detail prevents the buildup of corrosive debris that can damage glass over time. Enjoy a streak-free, panoramic view with our expert window maintenance.

Gutter Cleaning

Functional gutters are essential for directing rainwater away from your foundation and preventing expensive water damage. Our comprehensive cleaning service removes leaves, twigs, and sludge that can cause clogs and dangerous overflows. We don’t just clear the debris; we also flush the downspouts to ensure the entire system is flowing perfectly.

By maintaining clear gutters, you protect your landscaping, siding, and roofline from the weight of trapped water and debris. We take the "ladder work" off your hands, providing a safe and effective solution to keep your home’s drainage system in peak condition.

Common Questions

What areas do you provide pressure washing services for?

We proudly serve all of South Florida, including Palm Beach, West Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, North Palm Beach, and more. Our team handles both residential and commercial properties with the same level of care and professionalism.

What surfaces can you safely clean?

Canes Pressure Washing cleans a wide range of surfaces, including siding, roofs, driveways, patios, fences, windows, gutters, and even solar panels. We use the right combination of pressure washing or soft washing to safely remove dirt, mold, algae, and grime.

Are your cleaning solutions safe for my family, pets, and landscaping?

Yes! We use eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning solutions that are tough on dirt but safe for your family, pets, plants, and the environment. Our team carefully applies every solution to protect your property while delivering exceptional results.

How often should I have my home or business pressure-washed?

The frequency depends on your property type and local conditions, but most homes benefit from professional cleaning at least once a year. Commercial properties may require more frequent cleaning to maintain curb appeal and a welcoming environment for customers.

Do you offer free quotes and satisfaction guarantees?

Absolutely! Canes Pressure Washing provides free quotes for all residential and commercial services, and we stand behind our work with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. Our goal is to deliver reliable, professional results that exceed your expectations every time.
