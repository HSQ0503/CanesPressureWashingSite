  1. Information We Collect

    2. We may collect the following types of information:

    • Name, phone number, and email address
    • Service address or property details
    • Payment and billing information (if applicable)
    • Communication preferences

    We may also collect limited technical data such as:

    • IP address
    • Browser type
    • Pages visited on our website
  2. How We Use Your Information
    • Provide and manage our services
    • Respond to inquiries and customer service requests
    • Send appointment confirmations and service updates
    • Improve our website and customer experience
  3. SMS Communication & Consent

    4. By providing your phone number, you consent to receive SMS messages from Canes Pressure Washing. These messages may include:

    • Appointment reminders
    • Service updates
    • Customer support responses

    Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

    You can opt out at any time by replying:

    • STOP to unsubscribe
    • HELP for assistance
  4. SMS Consent & Data Sharing (IMPORTANT)

    5. We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

    6. Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

    • Improve user experience

    • Remember user preferences

    You can control cookies through your browser settings.

    7. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information, including:

    • Secure data storage

    However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

  7. Your Rights & Choices

    8. You have the right to:

    • Request access to your personal data
    • Request corrections or updates
    • Opt out of SMS communications at any time (reply STOP)
    • Request deletion of your data (where applicable)

    To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

  8. Third-Party Services

    9. We may use third-party service providers (such as payment processors, CRM systems, and messaging platforms) to operate our business. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their services and are required to safeguard your data.

Our Process

Reliable

We understand that your time is valuable. That's why we don't just "fit you in"—we arrive precisely when scheduled and stay until the job meets our rigorous standards. Reliability, to us, means consistency; whether it's your first service or your fiftieth, you'll receive the same high-caliber results. We treat every property with a level of stewardship and detail that reflects our deep respect for your investment, ensuring your home or business is in the safest possible hands.

Professional

Excellence is our baseline. Our team is not only highly trained in the latest industry techniques but is also equipped with state-of-the-art machinery and eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are tough on grime but gentle on the environment. Professionalism goes beyond the cleaning—it's in our clear, proactive communication, our uniformed presence, and the way we meticulously protect your landscaping and property. From the initial quote to the final walkthrough, we deliver a seamless, high-end experience.

Affordable

We believe that premium exterior care should be accessible, not a luxury. Our pricing model is built on transparency: you'll receive honest, upfront quotes with absolutely no hidden fees or "surprise" surcharges. We've optimized our processes to provide top-tier results at competitive rates, proving that you don't have to break the bank to maintain a pristine, high-value property. We make it simple and cost-effective to keep your curb appeal at its absolute peak year-round.
